My background spans:

- CAD workflows & low-voltage systems

This website serves as both a portfolio and an evolving lab, documenting projects, experiments, workflows, and ideas across engineering, drones, software, security, and emerging technology.

# 🛡️ Cybersecurity

## From Software to Silicon

I’m currently studying IT fundamentals and cybersecurity, working toward **CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+**, with a target of around **May 2027**. Practical labs are a core part of that process, alongside deeper study of Linux and networking.

My longer-term goal is to specialize in **hardware and IoT penetration testing**, focusing on:
- Embedded devices & firmware
- Drones & ground-control systems
- Embedded Linux
- Cyber-physical systems

The appeal is understanding a system all the way down, from the application to the operating system, firmware, and circuit board, and using that understanding to find and fix weaknesses. All of my testing is done on devices I own, in authorized engagements, or in isolated lab environments.

I wrote about this roadmap in more detail in [From Software to Silicon: My Roadmap to Hardware Hacking, IoT Security, and Cybersecurity Research](/from-software-to-silicon-cybersecurity-roadmap/).

## Looking Further Out

I’m also evaluating doctoral study in Cybersecurity, particularly programs that would support applied research into **embedded and cyber-physical systems security**. For now, my priority is hands-on experience and practical skill. A doctorate is a long-term possibility I’ll pursue only if the right research question takes shape.
