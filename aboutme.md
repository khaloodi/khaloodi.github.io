# ⚙️ Overwatch Technologies

## The Lab

Overwatch Technologies is my technology development lab: **software, AI, and cybersecurity, built, tested, and documented**. It’s where the skills I pick up through Project Khaled turn into real tools, systems, and experiments. It grew out of **Overwatch Drone Mapping**, my original aerial-data business.

### Overwatch OS: a probabilistic decision operating system

**[Overwatch OS](https://overwatch-os.vercel.app)** is a systems-engineering framework centered on probabilistic analysis, data tracking, risk management, and operational workflows.

It treats decision-making like an engineering system:
- Inputs
- Verification layers
- Risk constraints
- Calibration
- Feedback loops
- Long-term optimization

Rather than relying on emotion or isolated outcomes, it focuses on process integrity, statistical reasoning, data-driven iteration, and continuous refinement.

### Overwatch Engineering Lab: independent R&D

The Engineering Lab is my ongoing, self-directed practice of learning by building.

Current areas of focus:
- Mechanical design in Fusion 360 and Tinkercad
- Circuits and electronics fundamentals
- Hands-on electronics & embedded systems
- Systems integration and technical prototyping

The lab is where software, hardware, and security start to meet. Over time, more of its projects will include security from the ground up: designing a device, testing it in a controlled environment, and documenting how to harden it. Start with [Day 1: Learning Fusion on No Sleep](/overwatch-engineering-lab-day-1-fusion/).

### Cybersecurity research: from software to silicon

My longer-term goal is to specialize in **hardware and IoT penetration testing**, focusing on:
- Embedded devices & firmware
- Drones & ground-control systems
- Embedded Linux
- Cyber-physical systems

The appeal is understanding a system all the way down, from the application to the operating system, firmware, and circuit board, and using that understanding to find and fix weaknesses. Alongside certification study, I practice on hands-on platforms like TryHackMe and LetsDefend. All of my testing is done on devices I own, in authorized engagements, or in isolated lab environments.

I wrote about this roadmap in [From Software to Silicon: My Roadmap to Hardware Hacking, IoT Security, and Cybersecurity Research](/from-software-to-silicon-cybersecurity-roadmap/).

I’m also evaluating doctoral study in Cybersecurity, particularly programs that support applied research into **embedded and cyber-physical systems security**. For now, my priority is hands-on experience and practical skill.

### 🛰️ Overwatch Drone Mapping: aerial data

[Overwatch Drone Mapping](/drone-inspections-landing/) is where Overwatch started: bringing modern spatial technology into real-world applications through drone operations, photogrammetry, orthomosaic mapping, 3D modeling, and digital twin workflows.

It explores how unmanned aerial systems can become intelligent data collection tools for construction progress tracking, site documentation, property inspections, mapping and visualization, and infrastructure analysis.

> Transform aerial visuals into measurable, actionable information.

### 🚐 Overwatch Mobility Solutions: an exploratory concept

An early-stage idea, not an operating business: a future mobility and rental platform focused on modular adventure and mobile-workspace vehicles, combining remote work infrastructure, off-grid capability, smart storage, and connectivity and automation.
